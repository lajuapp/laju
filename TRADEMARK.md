# LAJU Trademark & Brand Policy

This document outlines the policy regarding the use of the **LAJU** name, logo, visual assets, and access to official production infrastructure.

---

### 1. Trademark Protection

The name **"LAJU"**, the official LAJU emblem/logo, application icons, and official color palettes are proprietary trademarks of the LAJU Project.

- **Forks and Derivative Builds**: If you modify the source code, create a public fork, or build your own application binaries from this repository, you **MUST** rebrand the application by removing all instances of the official LAJU name and logo. You may not distribute your derivative build under the name "LAJU" or any confusingly similar name.
- **Reference & Credit**: You may state that your product or service is *"Based on LAJU Community Edition"* or *"Compatible with LAJU protocols"*, provided it does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or official affiliation by the LAJU team.

---

### 2. Official Production Infrastructure Protection

The open availability of the source code does **NOT** grant access to official LAJU production infrastructure, including but not limited to:

1. Official push notification relay servers (APNs / FCM / Gotify production gateways).
2. Official WebRTC STUN/TURN traversal servers.
3. Official production API servers, media storage buckets, and user registries.

Third-party forks and self-hosted instances must configure and operate their own independent infrastructure endpoints.
